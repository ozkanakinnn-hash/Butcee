1. Screen recording: attached (minik-gurme-review.mp4). The developer does not own a physical iPhone, so the recording was captured on the iOS Simulator (latest iOS) running the submitted app code with live production services. It starts from app launch (a sample baby profile is pre-filled for the demo) and shows the typical flow: Today (meal diary), 100 Foods, Recipes, Guide, then the optional Parent Community: joining with a nickname and accepting the community rules (anonymous account, no e-mail or phone), writing a post, the Report dialog and the Block user dialog on another parent's post (cancelled in the video so as not to affect real users' content), opening a post, and finally Settings > Community > Delete my community account, which deletes the account and all of its posts and comments.

2. Purpose and audience: Minik Gurme helps parents of babies aged about 6-24 months start solid foods. It provides a 100-foods checklist with reaction notes, an allergen introduction plan with the 3-day rule, age-appropriate recipes, a month-by-month guide and WHO-based growth tracking. Target audience: parents and caregivers (adults). Value: one simple place to track what the baby has tried, spot reactions early and get age-appropriate ideas. All baby records stay on the device.

3. How to use: No login is required. On first launch enter a baby name, age and sex (any values) and tap Save; the Today screen opens. Bottom tabs: Today, 100 Foods, Recipes, Guide, Community. Reading the community needs no account; to write, tap Join, enter any nickname and accept the rules. Report and Block are in the menu (three dots) of each post and comment; blocked users can be managed in Settings > Community > Blocked users. Settings > Community > Delete my community account removes the account and all content. Sources of the information: Settings > Sources.

4. External services: Google Firebase (Authentication - anonymous accounts, and Cloud Firestore - community posts, comments, reports and moderation) for the optional community only. No advertising, no analytics, no AI services, no payment processing and no in-app purchases in this version.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in all regions; the interface and content are available in Turkish and English. The app is not distributed in China mainland.

6. Regulated content: the app is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat. It gives general infant-feeding information with cited sources (WHO, AAP and national health authority guidance, listed in Settings > Sources) and states clearly that it does not replace advice from a paediatrician. All texts, recipes and illustrations are our own; there is no protected third-party material. User-generated content: users can report posts and comments, block users, posts reported by several users are hidden automatically until a moderator reviews them, and users can delete their own content and community account at any time.
