2. Purpose and audience: Minik Gurme helps parents of babies aged about 6-24 months start solid foods. It provides a 100-foods checklist with reaction notes, an allergen introduction plan with the 3-day rule, age-appropriate recipes, a month-by-month guide and WHO-based growth tracking. Target audience: parents and caregivers (adults). Value: one simple place to track what the baby has tried, spot reactions early and get age-appropriate ideas. All baby records stay on the device.

3. How to use: No login is required. On first launch enter a baby name, age and sex (any values) and tap Save; the Today screen opens. Bottom tabs: Today, 100 Foods, Recipes, Guide, Community. Reading the community needs no account; to write, tap Join, enter any nickname and accept the rules. Report and Block are in the menu (three dots) of each post and comment; blocked users can be managed in Settings > Community > Blocked users. Settings > Community > Delete my community account removes the account and all content. Sources of the medical and nutrition information (with links to WHO, ESPGHAN, the Turkish Ministry of Health and AAP): the "Sources of this information" link at the bottom of the Guide, the 3-Day Rule / allergen plan, every food and recipe detail and the growth chart, the tappable "Source" line in the monthly guide, and Settings > Sources.

4. External services: Google Firebase (Authentication - anonymous accounts, and Cloud Firestore - community posts, comments, reports and moderation) for the optional community only. No advertising, no analytics, no AI services, no payment processing and no in-app purchases in this version.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in all regions; the interface and content are available in Turkish and English. The app is not distributed in China mainland.

6. Regulated content: the app is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat. It gives general infant-feeding information with cited sources (WHO, ESPGHAN, AAP and Turkish Ministry of Health guidance; each source is linked in the app and reachable from every screen that shows health information) and states clearly that it does not replace advice from a paediatrician. All texts, recipes and illustrations are our own; there is no protected third-party material. User-generated content: users can report posts and comments, block users, posts reported by several users are hidden automatically until a moderator reviews them, and users can delete their own content and community account at any time.
