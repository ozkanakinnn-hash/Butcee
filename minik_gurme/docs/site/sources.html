Sources

Last updated: 27.11.2026. Recipes, food guides, the allergen plan and growth charts in the app are based on the general recommendations of the sources below. These organisations have not reviewed or endorsed the app, and the content does not replace an individual assessment by your doctor.

Allergen labels

Recipe allergen labels follow the major allergens listed in Annex II of EU Regulation 1169/2011 and are derived automatically from ingredient names. Always check the labels of the products you use. eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/oj

Important

Every baby is different. If your baby has an allergy history, severe eczema, a chronic illness, was born prematurely or has feeding or swallowing difficulties, consult your paediatrician before following any recipe or plan. In an emergency (difficulty breathing, swelling of the face or lips, persistent vomiting) call 112.
