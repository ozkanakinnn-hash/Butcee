Account and data deletion

Baby records

Your baby's profile, meals, growth and allergy records are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. To delete them, use Settings › Data › Reset feeding statistics in the app, or uninstall the app.

Parent Community account

If you joined the community, Settings › Community › Delete my community account in the app immediately deletes your anonymous account and all posts and comments you wrote. You can also delete individual posts and comments from the ⋮ menu.

If you cannot access the app

If you uninstalled the app or cannot access your phone, e-mail ozkan.akinnn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my community account" and include your nickname and approximate posting dates. Your request is processed within 30 days and your community content is deleted.

Retained data

After deletion your community content and anonymous account are permanently removed. Reports other users made about your content are kept only until the moderation review is complete and are then deleted.
