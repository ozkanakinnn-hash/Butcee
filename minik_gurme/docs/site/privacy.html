Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12.11.2026. This policy explains how the Minik Gurme app (“the app”) handles your information.

Baby data stays on your phone

Everything you enter about your baby — name, age, sex, weight, height, meals, reactions, allergen trials and notes — is stored only on your device. It is never sent to our servers or to the Parent Community. The app has no analytics and no advertising.

Parent Community

Anyone can read the community without an account; posts are public within the app. If you choose to join the optional Parent Community, an anonymous account is created with Google Firebase (no e-mail or phone number). We then process: your nickname, the posts and comments you write, your likes, reports you submit and the time of each post. This content is visible to other community members. It is stored on Google Firebase servers in the EU (Frankfurt, europe-west3) and processed by Google as our data processor. Purpose: providing the community and keeping it safe (moderation). Legal basis: your explicit consent when joining (KVKK art. 5, GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). Content stays until you delete it; you can delete a post or comment at any time, and Settings › Community › Delete my community account removes all your posts, comments and the account. Do not share health details that identify you or your baby.

Health data

Weight, height, allergy and reaction records are health-related data. Because they never leave your device, they are not shared with us or any third party by the app. Whatever you write in the community yourself is visible to other members, so please avoid sharing sensitive personal information there.

Backups and files

If Android's own backup is enabled on your phone, Google may include the app's data in your device backup under Google's privacy policy. When you use “Export data” or “Doctor report”, a file is created only where you choose and is shared only with whom you choose.

Purchases

Minik Gurme Plus subscriptions are processed by Google Play. The app only receives whether a subscription is active; payment details are handled by Google under its own terms.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no data is sent anywhere.

Moderation and reports

Members can report content. Content reported by several members is hidden automatically and reviewed by our moderators, who may delete it. Reports (who reported, which content, the reason) are visible only to moderators.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers (18+), not for use by children.

Your rights

You can delete all data on your device at any time from Settings (reset/clear) or by uninstalling the app, and your community data from Settings › Community. Uninstalling the app does not delete community posts; delete your community account first. Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and GDPR you may request access, correction or deletion by contacting us.
