1. Screen recording: attached (haber-akisi-review.mp4). It was captured on a physical iPhone running the TestFlight build of this version (1.0.0, build 7) with live production data, starting from launching the app on the Home Screen. The app interface is in Turkish. It shows the typical flow: choosing interest topics on first launch, the news feed and categories, opening an article on the publisher's website inside the app, the market and weather panel, search, the notifications list, interests and settings. There is no account, login or user-generated content in the app.

2. Purpose and audience: Haber Akisi is a Turkish news aggregator for adult readers in Turkey. It collects headlines and short summaries from the public RSS feeds of Turkish news publishers into one clean, de-duplicated feed with clear source attribution, and sends only important breaking news as notifications. It solves the problem of following many separate sources and receiving too many notifications.

3. How to use: No account or login is required. On first launch choose any interest topics and tap Continue; the feed opens. Tap a headline to read the full article on the publisher's own website inside the app. Bottom tabs: Home (feed by category), Search, Notifications, Interests. The menu icon (top right) opens the market and weather panel and Settings. Settings: language (Turkish/English), theme, notification intensity (important only / balanced / all) and quiet hours. Football alerts: Interests > Football > choose teams.

4. External services: Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore for headlines, Cloud Messaging for notifications, Crashlytics, App Check); Google AdMob for ads (with Google UMP consent); public RSS feeds of news publishers (headline, short summary, link only); Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey (TCMB) indicative exchange rates and Truncgil Finans for gold prices; MET Norway for weather (CC BY 4.0); Highlightly and API-Football for match scores used in sports alerts. No AI services, no payment processing, no in-app purchases.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in all regions; the content is Turkish news and the interface is available in Turkish and English. The app is not distributed in China mainland.

6. Third-party material: the app shows only the headline, a short RSS summary (max 200 characters), the source name and a link; full articles open on the publishers' own websites and remain their property. Content is clearly attributed. Publishers can request removal at haberakisinfo@gmail.com and removed items are deleted from the feed. The app is not a regulated service and does not produce its own news content.
