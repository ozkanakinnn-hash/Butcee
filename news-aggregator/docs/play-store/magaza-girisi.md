```

📰 TEK AKIŞTA GÜNDEM
• Gündem, ekonomi, dünya, spor, teknoloji, sağlık ve daha fazlası

• Aynı haberin tekrarları ayıklanır, akış temiz kalır

## Tam açıklama — English (≤4000)
```
Haber Akışı brings headlines from Türkiye's leading news sources into one clean, fast feed. Pick your interests and get the news that matters to you.

📰 ONE FEED FOR THE NEWS
• Headlines, economy, world, sports, technology, health and more
• Full articles open on the publisher's own website, inside the app
• Duplicate stories are filtered out to keep the feed clean
• Search, source selection and multiple layouts

🔔 FEWER, MORE IMPORTANT NOTIFICATIONS
• Major breaking news confirmed by several sources
• Morning and evening "The day in 5 minutes" briefings
• "Who reported this?" on every story: the publishers covering the same event
• Earthquake alerts: magnitude 4.5+ instantly (source: AFAD)
• Choose your frequency: Important only, Balanced or Everything
• Quiet hours 00:00–08:00, except emergencies such as earthquakes
• One tap "Fewer notifications" button right on the notification

⚽ LIVE SCORE PANEL
• Live matches of your Süper Lig teams and the Turkish national team in your notifications
• Score and minute on the lock screen; expand for goals and cards
• Instant alerts for goals, red cards, half-time and full-time; kick-off whistle

💱 MARKETS AND WEATHER
• USD and EUR (Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye indicative rates), gold

🔒 RESPECTS YOUR PRIVACY

Haber Akışı is free and ad-supported. News content belongs to its publishers; the app shows only headlines, short summaries and links. Takedown requests: haberakisinfo@gmail.com

Market data is for information only and is not investment advice.

### Çekilecek ekran görüntüleri (telefonda, açık tema, bildirim çubuğu sade)
1. Ana akış (Gündem sekmesi, görselli haberler)
2. Kategori sekmesi (ör. Ekonomi) ya da ilgi alanı seçimi ekranı
3. Canlı skor paneli — bildirim alanında aşağı kaydırılmış hâli (goller/kartlar)
4. Sağ üst ☰ paneli: döviz, altın ve hava durumu
5. Bildirimler sekmesi
6. Ayarlar → Bildirim yoğunluğu (Sadece önemli / Dengeli / Tümü)
(İsteğe bağlı: koyu tema görünümü, arama ekranı)
