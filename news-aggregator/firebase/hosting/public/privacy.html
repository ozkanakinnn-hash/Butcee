Privacy Policy

Last updated: [[TARIH]] · Türkçe: Gizlilik Politikası (the Turkish version prevails)

Controller: [[AD_SOYAD]], [[ADRES]], email: [[EPOSTA]].

Summary

  • No account, no name, email, phone number or contacts are collected.
  • Your preferences (interests, followed teams, sources, read articles) are stored only on your device.
  • We do not sell personal data.

News websites

Articles open on the publisher's own website inside an in-app browser; those sites' cookies and data practices are governed by their own policies.

Ads and consent

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent via Google's consent form. You can change it in Settings → Privacy options or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Retention

On-device data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the cache. The server-side notification history (headlines only) is deleted after 7 days.

International transfers

Google and MET Norway process data outside Türkiye; see the Turkish KVKK notice for the legal basis.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Contact [[EPOSTA]] to exercise your rights under KVKK or, where applicable, the GDPR.
