## 5. Uygulama İnceleme bilgileri (App Review Information)

- **Oturum açma gerekli mi:** Hayır (hesap yok).
- **İletişim:** Ad, soyad, telefon ve e-posta (Apple yalnızca inceleme için kullanır).
- **Notlar (Notes)** — İngilizce yazın:
```
No account or login is required.
Location (When In Use) is used only to pick the user's city for prayer times, and to compute the qibla direction; the user can also choose a city manually.
The qibla compass needs a real device with a magnetometer; on the simulator it shows "Starting compass…".
Notifications are scheduled locally on the device for prayer times; no push server is used. Long-press a prayer notification to mark it as prayed.
The Live Activity (Lock Screen / Dynamic Island countdown) starts when the app is opened; it can be turned off in the Tracking tab.
Ads are served by Google AdMob; consent is collected with Google's UMP form where required, and the IDFA is used only after App Tracking Transparency permission.
```

### 5b. 4.3(a) reddine yanıt (App Review → Reply) — İngilizce
```
Hello,

Thank you for the review. We have reworked Ezan & Vakit around a feature set that is not a template prayer-times app:

1. Prayer tracking: users mark each of the five daily prayers (home screen ring, a "Prayed" tap on each time). A Tracking tab shows weekly / monthly views, streaks and per-prayer rates.
2. Automatic qada (missed prayer) log: on tracked days, unmarked prayers are added to the user's qada list, which they work down over time.
3. Live Activity: a live countdown to the next prayer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
4. Interactive widget (iOS 17): mark a prayer from the Home Screen without opening the app.
5. App Shortcuts / Siri: "Next prayer in Ezan & Vakit", "Mark prayed in Ezan & Vakit".
6. Actionable adhan notifications: "Prayed" and "Remind me in 20 min".
7. Prayer times are read from the official yearly district tables of Türkiye's Presidency of Religious Affairs, so they match to the minute.

We removed generic content that many apps share (greeting cards, the 99 Names list, kandil prayers module and the nearby mosques map) so the app focuses on tracking.

Screenshots of the new features are attached. Thank you.
```
